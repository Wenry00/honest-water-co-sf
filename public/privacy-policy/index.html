Privacy Policy

Last updated October 15, 2026

Golden Crest Water LLC (“Golden Crest Water,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates goldencrestwater.com . This Privacy Policy explains the types of information we may collect, how we use that information, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when requesting a quote, consultation, water assessment, appointment, installation, customer support, or other service.

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Home address, city, or ZIP code
  • Information about your water concerns or requested services
  • Appointment and installation information

We may also automatically collect certain technical information when you use our website, including device type, browser type, IP address, approximate location, pages visited, referring websites, and website interaction data.

How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected through our website, advertisements, lead forms, and communications to:

  • Respond to quote and consultation requests.
  • Schedule appointments and installations.
  • Communicate about our products and services.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Measure advertising and marketing performance.
  • Maintain business, accounting, tax, warranty, and service records.

SMS & Mobile Communications

If you separately consent to receive text messages from Golden Crest Water LLC, we may send messages relating to inquiries, appointments, service updates, promotions, offers, and other communications described when consent is provided.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe and HELP for assistance.

Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information sharing with subcontractors or service providers that support our operations, such as customer service or communications providers, may occur when necessary to provide those services. Text-message originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information

Golden Crest Water does not sell your personal information. We do not provide your personal information to unrelated third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

Service Providers

We may use service providers that help us operate our business, including customer relationship management platforms, website hosting, scheduling tools, communications providers, analytics providers, advertising platforms, payment processors, installation providers, and customer support services.

These providers may process information only as reasonably necessary to perform services on behalf of Golden Crest Water.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to operate the website, measure website activity, understand how visitors use our services, and evaluate advertising performance.

You may control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain customer relationships, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain business records.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may have certain rights under applicable privacy laws regarding personal information collected about them.

Depending on applicable law, these rights may include requesting access to certain personal information, requesting correction or deletion, obtaining information about how personal information is used, and exercising other privacy rights provided by California law.

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When changes are made, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will be revised.
