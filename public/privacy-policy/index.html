This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you. What we collect Information you provide. When you request a quote, consultation, water assessment, appointment, or other service, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, home address or ZIP code, and information you provide about your home or water concerns. Information collected automatically. Our website and advertising tools may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, IP address, referring website, and how you interact with our site. Advertising and analytics information. We may use advertising or analytics identifiers to understand which campaigns, ads, or website experiences result in inquiries or customers. Why we collect it To respond to quote, consultation, appointment, and service requests. To communicate about scheduling, installation, service, account updates, and customer support. To improve our website, marketing, customer experience, products, and services. To measure advertising performance and understand how customers find us. To maintain business, tax, accounting, warranty, and other records where required. SMS and mobile information If you separately opt in to receive text messages from Golden Crest Water LLC, we may use your mobile number to send the categories of messages described at the time you provide consent. Message frequency may vary, and message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. What we do not do We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing outside the purposes described in this policy and in any consent you provide. Service providers We may use service providers that help us operate the business, such as customer relationship management, scheduling, communications, hosting, analytics, advertising measurement, payment processing, and installation or customer-support services. These providers may process information only as needed to perform services for Golden Crest Water. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights. Cookies and tracking We may use cookies, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to operate the website, understand site usage, and measure advertising performance. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling some cookies may affect how parts of the site work. How long we keep information We keep inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary for customer follow-up and legitimate business purposes. Customer and transaction records may be retained longer when needed for service, warranty, tax, accounting, legal, or recordkeeping obligations. Your California privacy choices California residents may have rights under applicable privacy law to request information about personal information collected about them, request correction or deletion of certain personal information, and exercise other rights available under California law. Where applicable, you may also have the right to opt out of certain sale or sharing of personal information and to exercise your rights without unlawful discrimination. To submit a privacy request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests. Children Our website and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Security We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal information. No website, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be highlighted on our website where appropriate.
